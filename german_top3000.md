## How to Use This List

1. **Start from the top.** Words are ordered by frequency — learn them in this order.
2. **Master the first 100 words first** — they make up about half of all spoken German.
3. **Learn articles with nouns.** Every noun shows der/die/das — memorize them together.
4. **Practice in context.** Use each word in a sentence as you learn it.
5. **Use spaced repetition.** Import this CSV into Anki or similar apps.
