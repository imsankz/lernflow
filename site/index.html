A pipeline, not a course app

Duolingo sells you a closed loop. LernFlow is the open one: bring your own vocabulary, keep your deck forever, in standard Anki formats.

any wordlist CSV/TSV ─▶ lemma/gender/forms ─▶ AI translate (cached) ─▶ deck.tsv + deck.apkg
                                                            └────────▶ daily quiz (offline)

$ lernflow build wortliste.tsv --deck "Mein Deutsch" --apkg --audio
$ lernflow grammar data/banks/a1-grundlagen.yaml
$ lernflow quiz --n 8
🏠

Your machine, your data

Point AI_BASE_URL at Ollama, llama.cpp, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Nothing leaves your LAN unless you say so.

🛡️

Audit gate on every build

POS validity, gender sanity, duplicate & garbage-translation detection — fails fast with row numbers. No silent junk in your deck.

💾

Cached, deterministic, offline

Every translation cached; re-runs cost zero AI calls. The daily quiz is seeded — same seed, same questions, real retention measurement.

Your language is a PR away

A language wing is pure data — no code required. The pipeline already works for any language pair; German just got there first.

🌍

1. Grammar banks

Copy data/banks/a1-grundlagen.yaml, write clean-room items for your language, validate with lernflow grammar --no-ai. One A1 bank is a great first PR.

📚

2. Study guides

One CEFR level, one real exam — DELF, DELE, JLPT, TOPIK, CILS. The German B1 guides in docs/guides/ show the pattern.

🗂️

3. Web decks

A JSON array of cards for the PWA, from wordlists you're allowed to redistribute. Name the source and license; we track credits.
